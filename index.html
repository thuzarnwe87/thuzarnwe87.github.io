About

I am an ecologist currently working as a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Plant Ecology group at the Institute of Plant Sciences, University of Bern, Switzerland. My research focuses on the complex interactions between plants and soil organisms, particularly how their functional traits interact and how these relationships respond to environmental change. My work combines field research in natural and experimental grasslands, laboratory research, soil-fauna identification and trait measurements, ecological modelling, and statistical data analysis.

Research Projects

Fertilization & Collembola

Investigating the direct and indirect effects of fertilization on Collembola abundance and interactions with plant and fungal communities in natural grasslands.

Land-Use Intensification Through Time

Examining how the effects of land-use intensification on plant, fungal, and Collembola communities and their interactions change through time.

Plant-Fungi-Collembola Interactions Across Ecosystems

Comparing plant-fungi-Collembola interactions between natural and experimental grassland ecosystems.

Leaf Calcium & Collembola Functional Traits

Testing how leaf calcium variation and plant functional groups shape Collembola communities and functional traits in an independently designed common-garden experiment.
