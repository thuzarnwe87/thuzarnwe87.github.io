About

I am a Burmese ecologist from Myanmar currently working as a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Plant Ecology group at the Institute of Plant Sciences, University of Bern, Switzerland.

I am interested in the complex interactions between plants and soil organisms, particularly how their functional traits interact and how these relationships respond to environmental change.

My research combines grassland field experiments, laboratory work, soil-fauna identification and trait measurements, ecological modelling, and statistical data analysis.

Research Projects

Fertilization & Collembola

Investigating how fertilization affects Collembola abundance in natural and experimental grasslands.

Land-Use Intensification

Investigating how land-use intensification affects plants, Collembola, fungi, and their interactions through time.

Plant-Collembola Interactions

Comparing interactions between plant and Collembola communities in experimental and real-world grassland ecosystems.

Leaf Calcium & Functional Traits

My independent research project examines how leaf calcium variation and plant functional groups influence Collembola communities and functional traits.
